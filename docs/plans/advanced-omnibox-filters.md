
# Advanced omnibox filters: the `holds` and `fit` chips, their cards, the holds column and the fit diff

## Context
The omnibox answers "what do I own and where" and, since the abyssal work, "which of my mutated modules rolled well". It does not answer the two questions a pilot asks before undocking: "which of my ships are carrying enough X" and "which of my ships are fitted the way I meant them to be". Both are ship-scoped questions over a ship's *contents* (rows whose `location_id` is the ship's `item_id`), an axis no chip reaches today. This plan adds two chip kinds to the Assets omnibox -- `holds` (a count of one consumable inside a ship, whole-ship or per bay) and `fit` (equality of a ship's fitted modules against a pasted EFT fit, plus `is:fit`) -- each with a popover card in the abyssal card's shape, a temporary sortable count column for `holds`, and a fit-diff block in the inspector.

Grilled with the user on 2026-09-05; every decision below is settled.

## Settled decisions
- Both filters live inside the Assets omnibox as chip kinds with cards; no new tab, no window, no ESI scope (`SCOPES` untouched; ESI fittings import is a later item).
- **`holds`**: candidates are assembled ships only -- `a.is_singleton = 1` and SDE category Ship -- implied by the chip in both polarities, so no `cat:Ship` chip is needed. X is one exact SDE type name (case-insensitive) in this slice; a *group* form is a later follow-up with a seam left in the SQL. Two count forms: the summed total of everything sitting directly inside the ship (every `location_flag`: cargo, loaded charges in slot flags, fuel bay, ammo hold, drone/fighter bays, fleet hangar, every specialised hold), and three bay forms -- `cargo` (`Cargo`), `fuel` (`SpecializedFuelBay` + `SpecializedAmmoHold`), `drones` (`DroneBay` + `FighterBay` + `FighterTube0-4` + `FleetHangar`; the tubes ride with the bay because `fitting.py` already groups them under it). "Loaded in modules" is not a separate form. Operators are the `stat:` set (`<`, `<=`, `>`, `>=`, `=lo..hi`), reusing `parse_stat`/`StatTerm`/`_STAT_RE`. Ships with none of X count 0 and so match `<N`. Grammar: `holds:"Antimatter Charge M"<500`, `holds:"cargo/Antimatter Charge M"<500`, `holds:"fuel/…"`, `holds:"drones/…"`. Result: the ship rows plus a temporary sortable column with each ship's count of X, which leaves when the chip does.
- **`holds` negation** = the complement *within assembled ships*: `-holds:"Nanite Repair Paste">=100` lists the ships short of paste and nothing else. Justification: `stat:`/`roll:` negate as NOT EXISTS because an unfetched item has no value to compare and must survive; a ship always has a well-defined count (0 when empty), so there is no "no data" case to protect, and a NOT-EXISTS negation would list every non-ship asset in the estate alongside the short ships -- the opposite of useful. Both polarities therefore carry the ship restriction, mirroring `fit`'s hull-scoped negation.
- **`fit`**: fits are pasted as EFT text and stored under a name (from the `[Hull, Name]` header, editable) in a reusable list managed entirely inside the fit card (paste box, name, parse status, stored list, delete per entry). Match rule: the ship's fitted modules and rigs -- rows on `HiSlot*`/`MedSlot*`/`LoSlot*`/`RigSlot*`/`SubSystemSlot*` flags whose SDE category is not `Charge` -- must equal the fit's module + rig + subsystem multiset by exact `type_id`, slot order ignored. The fit's drone and cargo sections are not part of the verdict; they feed a "consumables short" line in the inspector diff. Hull scope: a fit compares only assembled ships of its hull, in both polarities; `-fit:"name"` lists the deviating ships of that hull only. Grammar: `fit:"name"`, `-fit:"name"`, `is:fit` / `-is:fit` = matches / does not match *any* stored fit for its hull; ships of hulls with no stored fit are excluded from both polarities of `is:fit`. Positive `fit:` chips OR (label family); positive `holds:` chips AND (comparison family, like `stat:`).
- **Cards**: `HoldsCard` (consumable picker with autocomplete over the types held inside the estate's ships with counts, any exact name typeable; bay selector All / Cargo / Fuel / Drones; operator and quantity, range form; live "N of TOTAL match") and `FitCard` (paste box, name, parse status incl. unknown names, stored list with per-entry delete, match / deviate polarity, live count). Both open on typing (Enter or the draft builder) one event turn after the chip lands, and from the chip's `▾` glyph; Done writes the chip, every other exit cancels. Three card kinds coexist through a registry; at most one is open.
- **Inspector** (panel and window) gains a fit-diff block for assembled ship rows: against the named fit when a positive `fit:` chip names a stored fit for the ship's hull, else the closest stored fit for the hull (fewest missing + extra, ties to the lowest `fit_id`); hidden when the hull has no stored fit. Lines: missing modules, extra modules, consumables short. Same shape as the rolled-stats block: hidden by default, per-host `AsyncQuery`, item guard.
- Saved views cover the new chips automatically via `to_text()`.
- **Amended 2026-09-05 -- five bays, SDE-wide drone and fighter lists.** The `drones` bay form shipped as `DroneBay` + `FighterBay` + `FighterTube0-4` + `FleetHangar`, which made "which carriers have fighters loaded" and "which ships have drones in the fleet hangar" unanswerable and listed a fleet hangar's crate as a drone. `fitting.HOLD_BAYS` is now `{"cargo": CARGO_FLAGS, "fuel": FUEL_FLAGS, "drones": ("DroneBay",), "fighters": ("FighterBay", "FighterTube0".."FighterTube4"), "fleet": ("FleetHangar",)}` in that order (the card's buttons after All: Cargo · Fuel · Drones · Fighters · Fleet); the grammar takes `holds:"fighters/…"` and `holds:"fleet/…"`, and the column header reads `Name · fighters`. `fitting.DRONE_FLAGS` keeps the wide set for `carried()` and the fit comparison, which deliberately count anything that launches from anywhere. `held_type_counts` vocabularies: `drones` = every published SDE type of category Drone (18) plus whatever sits in drone bays; `fighters` = every published type of category Fighter (87) plus the fighter bays and tubes; `fleet` = whatever sits in fleet hangars, any category, by name; `fuel` unchanged (SDE Fuel Block + Ice Product, unfaceted, plus the fuel and ammo holds). Every list that mixes the estate with the SDE puts the types the ships in scope actually hold first (units desc, then name) and the rest of the SDE after them by name; rows carry an `owned` flag so the card and the tests can tell the halves apart. The SDE halves are never faceted.

## Facts in the tree that shape the design
- `omni.py`: chip kinds compile in `FilterSpec.where`; `stat:`/`roll:` are correlated `EXISTS` (`_STAT_EXISTS`, `_ROLL_EXISTS`) negated as `NOT EXISTS`; `exclude_kinds` drops kinds in both polarities; `parse_stat` enforces the operator/range grammar; untranslatable chips are skipped silently; `_quote_value`/`_unquote` round-trip; `is:fitted` is `queries.HIDE_SHIP_CONTENTS_CLAUSE` with its polarity flipped by string replacement; `_IS_SQL` writes each pair out because naive `NOT(...)` is wrong for some flags -- `is:fit` joins that table.
- `assets` (db.py:280-310): `location_id` is the direct parent; `idx_assets_direct_loc` and `idx_assets_item` exist (the latter took `is:fitted` from 17 s to 7 ms over 25k stacks); `location_flag` is stored verbatim from ESI; a loaded charge shares its module's slot flag and is told apart by SDE category `Charge` (`fitting._render_slots`, `to_eft`). `ASSET_ROWS` does not project `location_id`; its inner aliases are `a, t, g, cat, mg, p, st, sr, rsys, sys, reg, ch, co` -- new subqueries must use fresh aliases. `FIT_ROWS`/`fetch_fit` return a ship's contents with `category`, `location_flag`, `quantity`.

## Pinned interface contracts (every package codes to these; deviations are reported, never improvised)

### db.py
- `SCHEMA_VERSION = 6`. Appended to `SCHEMA` after the `saved_views` block, before `http_cache`:
```sql
-- ------------------------------------------------------------- stored fits
-- EFT fits pasted into the fit chip's card. A `fit:` verdict is decided from
-- fit_items alone; eft_text is kept so the card can show and re-copy what was
-- pasted. Brand new tables, so CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS is the whole
-- migration (the pinned_labels precedent above). name carries NOCASE so the
-- chip's `f.name = ?` and the UNIQUE pair are both case-insensitive; the
-- UNIQUE autoindex (hull_type_id, name) also serves every hull lookup.
CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS fits (
    fit_id       INTEGER PRIMARY KEY AUTOINCREMENT,
    name         TEXT    NOT NULL COLLATE NOCASE,
    hull_type_id INTEGER NOT NULL,
    eft_text     TEXT    NOT NULL,
    created_at   TEXT    NOT NULL,
    UNIQUE (hull_type_id, name)
);

-- One row per (type, slot kind) carrying the multiset count: three Heat Sink
-- IIs are one row of quantity 3. slot_kind is 'module' | 'rig' | 'subsystem'
-- | 'drone' | 'cargo'; the first three are the verdict, the last two the
-- consumables line. A type resolves to exactly one kind (fits.classify).
CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS fit_items (
    fit_id    INTEGER NOT NULL REFERENCES fits(fit_id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
    type_id   INTEGER NOT NULL,
    slot_kind TEXT    NOT NULL,
    quantity  INTEGER NOT NULL,
    PRIMARY KEY (fit_id, type_id, slot_kind)
);
```
- `migrate()` gains a "v5 -> v6" comment block stating that both tables are brand new and nothing is rebuilt (keeping `SCHEMA` and `migrate()` in step per CLAUDE.md), and a test proves a v5 database (built from the current `SCHEMA` with the two `CREATE TABLE`s stripped, `schema_version` 5) has both tables and `schema_version` 6 after `init()`.

### fitting.py (flag vocabularies shared with the SQL)
- Module-level constants, built from the existing groups so the two can never drift: `FITTED_FLAGS: tuple[str, ...]` (HiSlot0-7, MedSlot0-7, LoSlot0-7, RigSlot0-7, SubSystemSlot0-7 -- the flags of `_SLOT_GROUPS`), `CARGO_FLAGS = ("Cargo",)`, `FUEL_FLAGS = ("SpecializedFuelBay", "SpecializedAmmoHold")`, `DRONE_FLAGS = ("DroneBay", "FighterBay", "FighterTube0".."FighterTube4", "FleetHangar")`, `HOLD_BAYS = {"cargo": CARGO_FLAGS, "fuel": FUEL_FLAGS, "drones": DRONE_FLAGS}` (dict order is the card's selector order after All). `CHARGE_CATEGORY = "Charge"` replaces the three string literals in `_render_slots`, `to_eft`, `to_esi_fitting`.
- `fitted_modules(rows) -> dict[int, int]`: `{type_id: count}` of a ship's fitted modules from `fetch_fit` rows (slot flags only, `Charge` category out, `quantity` summed) -- the Python twin of the SQL multiset, used by `fits.diff` and pinned equal to the SQL by test.

### fits.py (new, Qt-free; parser, store, diff)
- `SLOT_KINDS = ("module", "rig", "subsystem", "drone", "cargo")`; `VERDICT_KINDS = ("module", "rig", "subsystem")`.
- `@dataclass FitItem(type_id: int, name: str, slot_kind: str, quantity: int)`; `@dataclass ParsedFit(hull_type_id: int | None, hull_name: str, name: str, items: list[FitItem], charges: dict[int, int], unknown: list[str], empty_slots: int)` with `ok` property (`hull_type_id is not None and not unknown`) and `verdict_items()` / `consumable_items()`.
- `parse_eft(conn, text) -> ParsedFit`: header `[Hull, Name]` (name may be empty → `f"{hull} fit"`); a missing or malformed header → `hull_type_id None`, `hull_name ""`; strips a UTF-8 BOM, `\r`, trailing whitespace; ignores blank lines (section separators), `[Empty … slot]` lines (counted in `empty_slots`), and lines starting with `//` or `#`; `Name /OFFLINE` drops the suffix; `Name, Charge` is module `Name` with the charge tallied in `charges` (not stored -- EFT carries no charge quantity; a documented later item); `Name xN` is a stack of N (Pyfa's `x(?P<amount>\d+)`, no comma); anything else is one module. Names resolve via `SELECT type_id, group_id FROM sde_types WHERE name = ? COLLATE NOCASE ORDER BY published DESC, type_id LIMIT 1`; unresolved names land in `unknown` verbatim, never dropped. Items aggregate per `(type_id, slot_kind)` in first-seen order.
- `classify(conn, type_id, group_id, stacked: bool) -> str`: stacked lines → `"drone"` when the type's category is Drone (18) or Fighter (87), else `"cargo"`; unstacked → `"subsystem"` for category Subsystem (32), `"rig"` for category Module (7) with a group name starting `"Rig "`, else `"module"`. Category ids are CCP constants named in a comment; the lookup is by id, joined through `sde_groups`.
- Store (writes live here, the `stockpile.py` precedent): `save_fit(conn, parsed: ParsedFit, name: str, eft_text: str) -> int` (upsert by `(hull_type_id, name)`: an existing row is deleted first so the cascade clears its items; raises `ValueError` when `not parsed.ok`; `created_at` ISO UTC); `delete_fit(conn, fit_id)`; `list_fits(conn) -> list[sqlite3.Row(fit_id, name, hull_type_id, hull, modules, created_at)]` ordered hull then name NOCASE (`modules` = SUM of verdict-kind quantities); `fit_items(conn, fit_id) -> list[sqlite3.Row(type_id, name, slot_kind, quantity)]`; `fits_for_hull(conn, hull_type_id)`.
- `@dataclass FitDiff(fit_id, fit_name, named: bool, missing: list[tuple[str, int]], extra: list[tuple[str, int]], short: list[tuple[str, int, int]])` with `matches` property (`not missing and not extra`). `diff(ship_rows, items) -> (missing, extra, short)` is pure: `missing`/`extra` from `fitting.fitted_modules(ship_rows)` against the verdict items; `short` = `(name, have, want)` for every drone/cargo item whose `have` -- the type's summed quantity anywhere directly inside the ship, loaded charges included -- is below `want`. `diff_for_ship(conn, ship_item_id, hull_type_id, fit_name: str | None) -> FitDiff | None`: the named fit when `fit_name` names a stored fit for that hull (`named=True`), else the closest (fewest `len(missing) + len(extra)`, ties to the lowest `fit_id`); `None` when the hull has no stored fit. Reads through `queries.fetch_fit`.

### queries.py
- `SHIP_ROWS_CLAUSE = "(a.is_singleton = 1 AND cat.name = 'Ship')"` -- the one definition of "an assembled ship row", imported by `omni` for both chips and used by `count_ships`.
- `count_ships(conn, where, params, hull_type_id: int | None = None) -> int`: `count_assets` with `SHIP_ROWS_CLAUSE` (and `a.type_id = ?` when a hull is given) ANDed inside the same subquery -- the cards' TOTAL.
- `_sql_flags(flags) -> str` quotes a flag tuple into an `IN (...)` list; only `fitting.py` constants ever pass through it.
- `HOLDS_TYPE_IDS = "(SELECT type_id FROM sde_types WHERE name = ? COLLATE NOCASE)"` -- uncorrelated, evaluated once per statement; this string is the group-form seam (a later `group/` form swaps in a `sde_groups` lookup and nothing else changes). `holds_count_sql(bay: str | None) -> str`:
```sql
(SELECT COALESCE(SUM(h.quantity), 0) FROM assets h
 WHERE h.location_id = a.item_id AND h.type_id IN {HOLDS_TYPE_IDS}{bay_filter})
```
  with `bay_filter = ""` for the whole ship, else `f" AND h.location_flag IN ({_sql_flags(fitting.HOLD_BAYS[bay])})"`. One level only: a container's contents have the container's `item_id` as their `location_id` and are not counted (documented).
- `holds_counts(conn, ship_item_ids, type_name, bay) -> dict[int, int]`: the column's data, one `GROUP BY h.location_id` query per 900-id chunk (the `_abyssal_roll_rows` idiom), ships absent from the result read 0 in the model.
- `held_type_counts(conn, where, params) -> list[Row(type_id, name, units, ships)]`: the holds picker's vocabulary -- types sitting inside the assembled ships that the faceted `where` (written against `ASSET_ROWS`' aliases, injected inside the subquery like `abyssal_type_counts`) leaves, either on a non-slot flag or on a slot flag with category `Charge`; `units` = SUM(quantity), `ships` = COUNT(DISTINCT location_id); busiest first, then name NOCASE.
- `FIT_HULL = "f.hull_type_id = a.type_id"` and `FIT_EQUAL` (the multiset equality, correlated on `a.item_id` and `f.fit_id`):
```sql
(NOT EXISTS (
    SELECT 1 FROM assets fm
    JOIN sde_types fmt ON fmt.type_id = fm.type_id
    LEFT JOIN sde_groups fmg ON fmg.group_id = fmt.group_id
    LEFT JOIN sde_categories fmc ON fmc.category_id = fmg.category_id
    WHERE fm.location_id = a.item_id
      AND fm.location_flag IN ({fitted flags})
      AND COALESCE(fmc.name, '') <> 'Charge'
    GROUP BY fm.type_id
    HAVING SUM(fm.quantity) <> COALESCE((
        SELECT SUM(fi.quantity) FROM fit_items fi
        WHERE fi.fit_id = f.fit_id AND fi.type_id = fm.type_id
          AND fi.slot_kind IN ('module','rig','subsystem')), 0))
 AND NOT EXISTS (
    SELECT 1 FROM fit_items fi
    WHERE fi.fit_id = f.fit_id AND fi.slot_kind IN ('module','rig','subsystem')
    GROUP BY fi.type_id
    HAVING SUM(fi.quantity) <> COALESCE((
        SELECT SUM(fm.quantity) FROM assets fm
        JOIN sde_types fmt ON fmt.type_id = fm.type_id
        LEFT JOIN sde_groups fmg ON fmg.group_id = fmt.group_id
        LEFT JOIN sde_categories fmc ON fmc.category_id = fmg.category_id
        WHERE fm.location_id = a.item_id AND fm.type_id = fi.type_id
          AND fm.location_flag IN ({fitted flags})
          AND COALESCE(fmc.name, '') <> 'Charge'), 0)))
```
  The first direction catches extras and count differences from the ship's side, the second catches missing modules from the fit's side; both key `fm` on `idx_assets_direct_loc` and `fi` on the `fit_items` primary key. EXPLAIN expectation (pinned by test): the only `SCAN` lines are `SCAN a` (the outer table) and, for `holds:`, the uncorrelated `SCAN sde_types` of `HOLDS_TYPE_IDS`; `fm` and `h` resolve as `SEARCH … USING INDEX idx_assets_direct_loc`, `fi` as `SEARCH … USING PRIMARY KEY` (or the autoindex), `f` as `SEARCH f USING INDEX sqlite_autoindex_fits_1` or a covering index -- never `SCAN fm`, `SCAN h`, `SCAN fi`.

### omni.py
- `HOLDS_KIND = "holds"`, `FIT_KIND = "fit"`; `IS_FLAGS = ("fitted", "safety", "unpriced", "bpc", "fit")`; `Chip.kind` docstring lists both.
- `@dataclass HoldsTerm(bay: str | None, name: str, op: str, low: float, high: float | None)`; `parse_holds(value) -> HoldsTerm | None`: strips one leading `cargo/`, `fuel/` or `drones/` (case-insensitive; `fitting.HOLD_BAYS` keys) then applies `parse_stat` to the rest; an unrecognised prefix stays part of the name (forgiving, matches nothing). `holds_value(term) -> str` is the inverse (`"cargo/Antimatter Charge M<500"`, `"…=lo..hi"`); `holds_column_key(term) -> str` = `f"holds:{term.bay or 'all'}/{term.name.casefold()}"`; `holds_column_header(term) -> str` = `"Antimatter Charge M · cargo"` or the bare name for the whole ship.
- `parse`: `holds:<value>` mints `Chip(HOLDS_KIND, value)` only when `parse_holds(value)` is not None (the `stat:` rule; otherwise bare text); `fit:<value>` mints `Chip(FIT_KIND, value)` for any value including a quoted empty one (the level rule); `is:fit` mints `Chip("is", "fit")`. `to_text` needs no special case (`_quote_value` quotes both). Neither `holds` nor `fit` is a bare-word chip.
- `where`: `holds` positive → `({SHIP_ROWS_CLAUSE} AND {holds_count_sql(bay)} {cmp})`, negated → `({SHIP_ROWS_CLAUSE} AND NOT ({holds_count_sql(bay)} {cmp}))`, params `(name, *numbers)`, several positives AND; `fit` positive chips OR together as `({SHIP} AND (EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM fits f WHERE f.name = ? AND {FIT_HULL} AND {FIT_EQUAL}) OR …))`, each negated chip → `({SHIP} AND EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM fits f WHERE f.name = ? AND {FIT_HULL} AND NOT {FIT_EQUAL}))`; `_IS_SQL["fit"] = (positive, negative)` with positive `({SHIP} AND EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM fits f WHERE {FIT_HULL} AND {FIT_EQUAL}))` and negative `({SHIP} AND EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM fits f WHERE {FIT_HULL}) AND NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM fits f WHERE {FIT_HULL} AND {FIT_EQUAL}))`. Only whitelisted operators reach the SQL text; names and numbers are bound.
